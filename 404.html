SRM InnovateX 2026 is a student-driven 24-hour hackathon designed to bring together developers, designers, innovators and aspiring entrepreneurs to build practical technology solutions for real-world challenges.

Mission
Empower students to solve real-world problems through code and creativity.
Vision
Build India's most vibrant student innovation ecosystem.
What you'll experience
Innovation
Identify problems, ideate solutions, and build working prototypes.
Collaboration
Team up with the brightest builders across India.
Mentorship
Interact with industry professionals and startup founders.
Opportunities
Discover internship and career pathways with partners.
Multi-Stage Evaluation
First 20 min
Problem Pitch

Prototype Review

Industry Judging

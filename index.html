SRM InoVateX 2026 is a student-driven 24-hour hackathon designed to bring together developers, designers, innovators and aspiring entrepreneurs to build practical technology solutions for real-world challenges.

Mission
Empower students to solve real-world problems through code and creativity.
Vision
Build India's most vibrant student innovation ecosystem.
Multi-Stage Evaluation
Problem Pitch
First 20 min
Define your problem and present your approach.
Progress Check

Prototype Review

Shortlisted teams pitch to founders and experts.
